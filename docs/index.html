q-edit

A small, fast desktop video editor for Windows.

Source code Terms of Service Privacy Policy

q-edit lets you cut and combine videos, images and audio on a multi-track timeline, adjust each clip in a DaVinci-style inspector, and export or post the result straight to TikTok. It runs entirely on your own computer.

View on GitHub

Features

  • Multiple video and audio tracks with cutting, splitting, trimming, snapping and undo
  • Drag and drop media from Explorer; thumbnails and waveforms on the timeline
  • Per-clip zoom, position, rotation, cropping, opacity, fades, volume and pan
  • Crop to any aspect ratio and export with presets, including GPU encoding
  • Post to TikTok with a caption, cover image and on-video text

Posting to TikTok

q-edit uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. You log in to TikTok in your browser, choose who can see each video, and nothing is posted until you press Post. See the Privacy Policy for what the app can access.

q-edit is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
