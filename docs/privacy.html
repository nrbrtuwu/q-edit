Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

q-editTerms of Service

q-edit is a desktop video editor that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information the app handles, in particular when you connect a TikTok account.

No servers, no tracking

q-edit has no servers of its own. It does not collect analytics, does not track you, and does not send your videos or personal information to the developer or to anyone else, except to TikTok when you choose to post a video.

Your videos

Videos, images and audio you edit stay on your computer. A video is uploaded only when you press Post in the Post to TikTok panel, and only to TikTok.

Information from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, you log in on TikTok's own website and approve the permissions q-edit asks for:

  • user.info.basic: your account's open ID, display name, username and profile picture, used to show which account you are posting to.
  • video.publish: lets q-edit post the videos you choose, with the caption, cover and privacy settings you pick, and check whether a post has finished processing.

q-edit also reads your account's posting options (such as allowed privacy levels and the maximum video length) right before you post, as TikTok requires.

Storage

The TikTok login tokens and the app keys you enter are stored only on your computer, in your Windows user profile, encrypted with Windows Data Protection so that only your Windows account can read them. App settings (such as export preferences) are stored in the same folder.

Sharing

Information is shared only with TikTok, and only to log in and to post the videos you choose. TikTok's handling of your data is covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Removing your data

  • Click Disconnect in the Post to TikTok panel to revoke q-edit's access and delete the stored login from your computer.
  • You can also remove q-edit's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Uninstalling q-edit and deleting the %APPDATA%\q-edit folder removes all of its local data.

Children

q-edit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
